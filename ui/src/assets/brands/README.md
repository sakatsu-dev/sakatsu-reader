## Terms that decide how they are used

- **GitHub** (`https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo`): the Invertocat appears only in white,
  black, or in a few cases grey or green, and is never distorted or altered. It stays less
  prominent than the product's own name and must not imply GitHub's endorsement. So the page shows
  the black mark on a light theme and the white one on a dark theme, at text size, after the
  masthead.
- **Ko-fi** (`https://more.ko-fi.com/brand-assets`): Ko-fi offers its logos, buttons and cups for
  pointing people at a Ko-fi page. Its downloadable asset archive sits behind a browser challenge
  and could not be fetched by a script, so the cup comes from Ko-fi's own storage instead: the
  image its official button embeds. It keeps its own colours (white cup, dark outline, red heart),
  which read on both themes.

These are trademarks of their owners and are used only to label links to the creator's own
GitHub and Ko-fi pages.
